# Tune privacy policy

Tune uses the device microphone only while its tuner screen is visible. Audio is processed in memory on the device to estimate musical pitch. Tune does not record audio to a file, collect personal data, use analytics, or transmit data over a network. The app requests microphone permission so it can show the pitch of a played guitar string.

To stop microphone access, leave the app or revoke microphone permission in Android settings.
